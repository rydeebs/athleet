GOOD QUESTIONS

Before the
starting gun.

Who is Athleet for?

Endurance athletes with a credible race calendar, an engaged audience, and visible sponsorship opportunities. From HYROX and 5Ks to half marathons, triathlons, and ultramarathons, each profile can show the discipline, distance, date, and audience for every race.

What does a brand actually get?

A defined body or kit placement, agreed athlete social posts, and photo proof from the event. Each package sets out the format, placement, race dates, and deliverables in advance.

Can I sponsor a whole season?

Yes — the concept supports both single-race packages and a season of agreed events. Athletes and brands agree the schedule and deliverables upfront.

Are all tattoos and logos allowed at events?

Rules vary by organizer, venue, and event. Athletes and brands need to confirm permitted placements and any existing sponsorship restrictions before agreeing a package.

Do athletes need to wear a GPS tracker?

No. The first version is built around placements, posts, and event photo proof. Venue-level retargeting is a possible future add-on and is not included in these packages.
