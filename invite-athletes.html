What does accepting this invitation mean?

You’ll create a real enduur account and can build your profile and publish race listings. Joining doesn’t commit you to a sponsorship or guarantee a match. This is an early product, and we’re learning with you.

Are payments live yet?

Not yet. You can set up your profile and explore partnerships now. Checkout and payouts are not enabled, so no paid booking can be completed yet. No card or bank details are required to join.

Do I need a body scan or a big following?

No. Use a generic avatar, choose the placements you’re comfortable with, and share your race plans and results. A photo likeness is optional. Brands can consider your audience alongside your event and performance.

How do I share feedback?

Reply directly to the person who sent your invitation. Tell us what you were trying to do, what worked, and where you got stuck.
