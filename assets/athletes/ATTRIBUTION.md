# Athleet generic avatar library — version 1

The body mesh, morph targets, skin textures, eyes, hair and shoes derive from the MakeHuman community's explicitly **CC0** core assets. No athlete was scanned and no person's likeness is claimed. These are adult generic placement templates, not measurements or digital twins.

Sources:
- [MakeHuman licensing](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/about/license.html)
- [CC0 system asset pack](https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html)
- [MPFB source](https://github.com/makehumancommunity/mpfb2/tree/afb9f530a7c2741dedb8df0ebae2e0b183caec21/src/mpfb/data)
- [CC0 1.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode)

Source asset headers name Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier as copyright holders at the September 2020 CC0 release. Athleet's fitted garments, normalized templates, export pipeline and rendered posters are generated by `scripts/build-athletes.py`. MPFB application code is not bundled. Three.js is distributed under its MIT license (included by the bundler).

Included source assets: hm08 base; young adult masculine/feminine macro and muscle/weight targets; young light/dark skin textures; short02 / ponytail01 hair; low-poly eyes with brown texture; shoes04. Facial geometry blends the three source ancestry targets equally, independently of the user's skin-tone choice. No ethnicity is inferred or collected.
