Clear terms.
Shared expectations.

These rules apply to new bookings accepted under this version. The booking records the athlete’s race, placement, price, artwork instructions, deliverables, and both parties’ agreement. There is no paid booking until the portal confirms payment is secured.

A funded booking

The sponsor has 48 hours after acceptance to pay. An unpaid reservation expires. Do not begin paid work until the booking shows Payment secured.

The agreed work

The confirmed placement, artwork instructions, deliverables, and deadlines form the brief. Required pre-publication content approval is agreed before booking. No new requirements can be added unilaterally.

Proof and review

Submit placement photos and live post links within 7 days after the race. Sponsors have 72 hours after the proof-review email notification to approve or report a specific unmet requirement. Silence makes the booking eligible for automatic release. Bank arrival follows Stripe’s payout schedule.

Objective approval

Low engagement, sales, or finish position are not grounds to withhold the base payment. A DNF is reviewed against actual participation and delivery, not an implied finish guarantee.

Changes and cancellations

Either party can request review for cancellation, injury, withdrawal, a postponed race, or missed deliverables. Undelivered work is refundable; completed work is assessed against the agreed brief and allocation. Moving to another race requires a new agreement by both parties.

Partial delivery

The booking records separate placement and social-content values. Enduur reviews evidence and releases the earned amount, with the corresponding markup retained, and refunds the rest. A timely issue pauses automatic release.

Price and payout

Athletes set their requested payout. Sponsor prices include a 20% enduur markup on that amount. Stripe costs come from enduur’s share. No payment-processing charge is added at checkout. Amounts are USD; personal taxes remain each party’s responsibility.

Single races first

Each payment covers one race and one placement. Season plans require separate race bookings. Delayed transfers are not an escrow service.

Pilot timing and payment support

US-based athletes receive USD through a verified US Stripe payout account. Accept bookings 1–30 days before race day. Payment is due within 48 hours of acceptance, or before race day begins in UTC, whichever is sooner. Race dates and proof deadlines use UTC; the portal displays deadlines in your local timezone.

Complete evidence is due by the end of the seventh day after the race (UTC). If official photos arrive later, request a review before that deadline so both parties can agree an extension. Missing proof pauses payout for manual review; it does not automatically forfeit all earned payment.

Use “Report issue / cancellation” in the partnership before the review deadline. The review clock starts when the email provider accepts the proof notification. If sending fails, automatic release waits. Enduur reviews the agreed allocation and evidence from both parties. Any partial resolution includes an explanation and retains only the markup corresponding to earned work. Payment disputes and unusual provider errors require manual review. Do not begin new work while the payment is paused.

For help, including settled payments or a bank payout problem, email accounts@enduur.co with your booking ID. Required consumer rights and payment-provider dispute processes are unaffected by the review window. Refund bank arrival depends on your payment provider.

Secure payment processing by Stripe. A transfer to an athlete’s Stripe account and arrival in their bank account are separate steps.
