What does accepting this invitation mean?

You’ll create a real enduur account and can build your brand profile, explore published listings, and send partnership requests. Joining doesn’t commit you to a sponsorship or guarantee a match. This is an early product, and we’re learning with you.

Are payments live yet?

Not yet. You can set up your profile and explore partnerships now. Checkout and payouts are not enabled, so no paid booking can be completed yet. No card or bank details are required to join.

What can a sponsorship include?

An agreed logo on the athlete’s body or kit, social posts, and photo proof from the race. Placement availability depends on the athlete’s clothing, preferences, and event rules. Scope and pricing are agreed per partnership.

How do I share feedback?

Reply directly to the person who sent your invitation. Tell us what you were trying to do, what worked, and where you got stuck.
