A race partnership.
Not a map order.

The distinction is where you start—and what you can prove.

  1. 01

    Start with a body placement.

    The athlete, their race and their audience come first. A venue-only media buy starts with a polygon.

  2. 02

    Own your category in our campaign.

    The proposed lock covers participating Athleet placements and our follow campaign. Anyone can target the same hall elsewhere. We don’t claim to own the venue.

  3. 03

    Measure actions beyond attendance.

    Tattoo QR scans, athlete-link clicks and code redemptions add first-party signals to “devices at the venue.” They are separate measures, not proof that every device saw the tattoo.

  4. 04

    Build from people who engage.

    Later, with enough eligible QR and code conversions, build a lookalike from people who engaged with sponsored athletes—not everyone at the expo. Planned, subject to consent and platform minimums.
